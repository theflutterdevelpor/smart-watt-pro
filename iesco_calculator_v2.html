📐 Formulas reference (all categories)
A. ENERGY CHARGES (depends on tariff method) Slab: sum of (units_in_slab × slab_rate) for each slab Flat: total_units × flat_rate ToU: OP_units × OP_rate + PK_units × PK_rate B. FIXED CHARGES (depends on tariff) Per phase: Rs 75 (1-φ) / Rs 150 (3-φ) [residential] Flat: Rs 1000 / 2000 / 50000 etc. [other] Per MDI kW: MDI × Rs 1250 [B2b/B3/B4 industrial] C. ADJUSTMENTS VarFPA = prev_month_units × notified_FPA_rate (back-billed) QTA = current_units × QTA_rate (quarterly) D. TAX BASE & GOVT CHARGES FC_Surch = units × Rs 3.23 NJ_Surch = units × NJ_rate TaxBase = Energy + Fixed + QTA + FC_Surch (VarFPA excluded) GST = TaxBase × 18% Further Tax = TaxBase × 4% Extra Tax = TaxBase × 17% Income Tax = TaxBase × IT% FPA_Taxes = VarFPA × (GST% + IT% + FT% + ET%) E. TOTAL Payable = IESCO + Govt AfterDue = Payable × (1 + LPS%)
